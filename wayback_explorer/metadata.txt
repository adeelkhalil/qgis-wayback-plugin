about=Wayback Explorer streams historical Esri World Imagery releases straight from the official Esri Wayback WMTS
    service into QGIS. Scrub a timeline across every published release (2014 to today) and add any of them to the
    project, or keep a live layer that follows the slider. Imagery is streamed on demand through the QGIS WMTS
    provider only: the plugin never bulk-downloads tiles and never caches imagery to disk, in line with the Esri
    Terms of Use. Loaded layers carry the required attribution and an attribution badge is shown on the map canvas.
    Imagery credit: Esri World Imagery (Wayback). Source: Esri, Vantor, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User
    Community. Wayback: https://livingatlas.arcgis.com/wayback/ Terms of Use:
    https://www.esri.com/en-us/legal/terms/full-master-agreement This plugin is not affiliated with or endorsed
    by Esri.
